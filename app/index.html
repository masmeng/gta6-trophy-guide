    Roadmap to the Platinum

    About 200 hours, one playthrough, no difficulty settings to worry about. Follow the three steps in order.

    The 100% completion checklist, summarised

    After the credits, the checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It has 35 requirements in three groups. Finish the story; rob all 39 gang operations, all 22 bank jobs and all 7 Payday Loan jobs; find all 33 underwater discoveries; earn gold in all 42 Shooting Range challenges; win all 42 drone races; and finish all 15 of Bret’s Target Decks. The partial targets are gentler: 100 optional tasks, 34 of 35 Smash ’N’ Grabs, 50 of 91 stick-ups, fence $100,000 of narcotics, $100,000 of jewelry, $250,000 of marked bills and $200,000 of vehicles, 300 of 397 stunts, 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 15 of 18 meditation spots, 15 of 18 card sets, 100 of 125 Cocaine Flamingos destroyed, any 70 of the 80 challenges, 100 WAiNK locations, 30 of 40 race wins and 10 of 13 race-challenge gold medals.

    Missable Trophies

    Every other trophy can be earned in free-roam after the story. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep manual saves at the start and end of each chapter as insurance — 15 manual slots plus one autosave are available.
