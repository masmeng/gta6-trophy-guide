Leonida Trophy Guide

Roadmap to platinum — ~200 hours

  1. Stage 1 · The story 60–80 hours

    Play the 5 chapters, do the side-character questlines as they appear, scan vehicles and study animals as you go, and meditate at every spot. Everything in this stage is available during the story — except one meditation in Chapter 1 (see the warning below).

  2. Stage 2 · The 100% checklist ~100 hours

    After the story, the checklist opens under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient — about 60% true completion — but it is the long grind: stunts, races, ranges, cards, challenges and every robbery.

  3. Stage 3 · Cleanup ~10 hours

    Strangers & Freaks strands, the last few miscellaneous trophies, and the four trophies whose unlock method the community has not confirmed yet. Then the platinum.

⚠ The one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude — the first meditation can only happen in Chapter 1 at Jason’s house. After the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. It is not yet clear whether that first meditation counts toward the trophy, so do it while you can. No other trophy is missable, and cheats disable trophies, so leave them off.

No online trophies

All 55 trophies are offline single-player. There is nothing to earn in multiplayer, and no DLC trophy is required for the platinum.
